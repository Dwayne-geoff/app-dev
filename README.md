# My Favorite Movie

## Spider-Man: No Way Home

![Spider-Man](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/0/00/Spider-Man_No_Way_Home_poster.jpg)

### About the Movie
Spider-Man: No Way Home is a superhero movie about Peter Parker, whose identity is revealed to the world. He seeks help from Doctor Strange, but things go wrong, bringing villains from different universes.

### My Favorite Characters
- Spider-Man (Peter Parker)
- Doctor Strange
- Green Goblin
- Doctor Octopus

### Why I Like This Movie
I like this movie because of its amazing action scenes, story, and the appearance of different Spider-Man characters.

### My Rating
**10/10**
